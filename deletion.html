Data Deletion Instructions

Zaygal Studio · Effective 24 October 2026

You can ask Zaygal Studio to delete any personal data we hold about you, including WhatsApp message records and your phone number.

How to request deletion

  1. Email dosunmuh070@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address you used with us, or include the WhatsApp number you messaged us from, so we can find your records.
  3. You can also reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from us to stop further messages immediately.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt within 3 working days.
  • We delete the records within 30 days, unless we are legally required to keep them, for example tax records.
  • We email you when the deletion is complete.
  • Records held by Meta under its own policies are deleted through Meta's own tools; we will tell you how to request that if it applies.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number and WhatsApp profile name from our contact records.
  • Message content and conversation history we stored.
  • Any notes or files you sent us that are not needed for an active contract.

Full details of what we hold and why are in our Privacy Policy.
