Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Zaygal Studio ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use our websites, contact us, or exchange messages with a WhatsApp number we operate. We are based in Abuja, Nigeria, and we process data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, organisation, email address, phone number.
  • Message data when you message a WhatsApp number we operate: your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, the content of messages you send us, timestamps, delivery and read status, and template identifiers where a conversation starts from a template.
  • Technical data from our website and messaging infrastructure: IP address, browser type, pages requested, and error logs.
  • Payment and billing data if you become a customer. Card details are handled by our payment processor, not stored by us.

We do not collect special category data, and we do not ask for identity documents through WhatsApp.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiries and provide services you request.
  • To send you updates about work you have asked us to do, including through WhatsApp where you have contacted us first or opted in.
  • To operate, secure and debug our systems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We rely on your consent for marketing messages, on performance of a contract for work you request, and on our legitimate interest in operating and securing our services. You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to a WhatsApp message or emailing us.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

Our WhatsApp messaging is delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta. When you message us, Meta processes the message on our behalf and also acts as an independent controller for its own services under its own privacy terms. Meta may process data outside Nigeria. We only message people who have contacted us or opted in, and we honour opt-out requests immediately.

4. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Meta Platforms, to deliver WhatsApp messages.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run our website and servers.
  • Professional advisers, where needed for legal or accounting purposes.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your phone number with advertisers.

5. Retention

Message records are kept for up to 24 months from the last exchange, then deleted or anonymised. Enquiry records are kept for up to 24 months. Billing records are kept for as long as tax law requires, currently six years. Website logs are kept for up to 90 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send you a copy. Email dosunmuh070@gmail.com with the subject line Data request. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Security

Access to messaging systems is limited to authorised staff, credentials are stored encrypted, and connections to the WhatsApp Business Platform use tokens issued by Meta. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these measures seriously and review them.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has messaged us, contact us and we will delete the record.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, tell you directly.
